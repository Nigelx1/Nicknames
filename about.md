# Nicknames

Rename how other players show up in Geometry Dash. Type a nickname once and it replaces their username everywhere you'd normally see it.

Nothing leaves your game. The other person never finds out, nobody else sees it, and nothing is sent anywhere — it's a label on your screen and only your screen.

## Where the rename shows up

- **Level leaderboards** — top 100, friends, weekly, all of them
- **Comments** — both level comments and profile posts
- **Friends list and user search**
- **The profile page itself**

## Renaming someone

Open their profile and hit the extra button in the left-hand column, next to info and chat. Type whatever you want and save.

To undo it, open the same popup and press **Reset** — they go back to their real username immediately.

While a nickname is active, their real username still shows on their profile in small gray text underneath, so you never lose track of who you're actually looking at.

## What it doesn't touch

Only the text being drawn on screen changes. The underlying account data is left completely alone, which means:

- Tapping a name still opens the right profile
- Messaging, friend requests and profile links all still work normally
- Scores, comments and everything else stay attached to the real account

Nicknames are stored against **account IDs**, not usernames — so if someone changes their GD name, your nickname for them survives it.
